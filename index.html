🚨 Cut the grapes and any cherry tomato in half lengthwise — if either ends up on the table. Whole grapes are the number-one choking food for under-4s and the room is full of three-year-olds. No whole nuts anywhere.
🧊 Cut fruit needs a cooler, not a table. Melon and citrus sitting out from half two until dinner is four hours in this heat — keep it boxed and cold until the snack table opens at quarter to six.
🥄 Dips want a spoon each, not shared crisps. Thirty-odd people double-dipping one tub is the other thing that goes wrong. Decant into small bowls.
